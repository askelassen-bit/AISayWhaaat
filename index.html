Shop Floor Item Designer

Create frontal views of shop floor items and configure markers.

How it works

  • 1 pixel equals 1 cm on the canvas.
  • Drag markers around; they stay inside the item.
  • Stripe marker positions are relative to the item bottom-left corner.
  • Chessboard marker positions are relative to their stripe marker.
Fill in the form and click “Create New Shop Floor Item” to begin.
